Junior leans toward easier, more rideable surfaces and simpler shapes. Pro pushes harder for maximum unpaved/trail and more technical routing. Adjusts the minimum-unpaved slider above — feel free to fine-tune it afterward.
Loop
Crisscross
Figure
Loop sweeps around the start in one steady circuit. Crisscross visits the same via-points in a scrambled order instead — cutting back and forth through the middle rather than tracing a clean perimeter, so it turns up more small/unexpected roads. Figure traces a chosen shape — it'll only ever roughly resemble it, since the route still has to follow real roads.
⚡ Fast
Detailed
Detailed anchors the route on real trail data from several sources and tries several shapes before settling — best off-road quality, a bit slower. Fast skips that groundwork and takes the first result — noticeably quicker, at the cost of less-refined trail selection and distance accuracy.
Drag Route: grab the line anywhere and drag it — release to snap onto the nearest real, rideable road and re-route just that section. Select Segment (experimental): tap a start point then an end point — that stretch is immediately cleared and locked in, and you can drag anywhere inside it right away, no other toggle needed. Drags inside a selected segment prefer unpaved ground; the rest of the route stays protected.
